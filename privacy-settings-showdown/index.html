Privacy Settings Showdown

Can you lock down a profile without hiding the harmless stuff?

  • You will lock down 3 made-up social profiles, one at a time. Each one gets a little harder.
  • Set every detail to Public, Friends, or Hidden. Then fix risky settings like location sharing, who can message you, and tagged photos.
  • Tap Lock it in to see your safety score and a short note on why each detail matters.
  • Hiding everything is not a perfect score. A favorite team is fine to share. A pet’s name, a full birthday, or an “away on vacation” post is not.
  • On a keyboard: Enter starts, locks in, and continues.

Every person, place, and phone number in this game is made up. Phone numbers use the 555 prefix only as practice. This is not a real social network.
