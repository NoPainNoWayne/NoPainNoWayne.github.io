Fake Shop Finder

Can you tell a real store from a holiday scam shop?

  • 10 pretend store pages appear, one at a time, and a short timer is running.
  • Tap any red flags you spot: a price that is way too good, payment only by gift card, wire, or crypto, missing contact or return info, a lookalike or odd web address, reviews that all say the same thing, a fake countdown, a brand-new site, or a shop that only exists in social media ads.
  • Then tap Real Shop or Scam Shop. Real stores have fair prices, ordinary card payment, and a way to get in touch — not every page is a scam, so leave those unmarked and choose Real Shop.
  • You score points for the right call and for each flag you catch, plus a speed bonus and a streak bonus. After each page, a tip explains the giveaways.
  • On a keyboard: R or ← Real Shop, S or → Scam Shop. You can also swipe the page: left Real Shop, right Scam Shop. Press Enter to start.

Every store, product, and web address in this game is made up for practice. Addresses are display-only and cannot be opened. Phone numbers use the 555 exchange.
