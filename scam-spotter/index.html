Top scam red flags

  1. Urgent threats — “act now or your account closes.”
  2. Odd or misspelled sender addresses.
  3. Asks for gift cards, a wire, or crypto.
  4. Unexpected links or “just call this number.”
  5. “Don’t tell anyone” or “don’t call me back.”
