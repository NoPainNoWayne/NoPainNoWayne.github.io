Text Message Triage

Can you sort a risky text before it tricks you?

  • 12 messages land in your inbox, one at a time. A short timer is running.
  • Tap Safe if you can trust it, Scam if it is a trick, or Check First if you should verify another way before you act.
  • Faster answers and streaks score more. After each choice, a tip explains the tell.
  • On a keyboard: A or ← Safe, C or ↑ Check First, D or → Scam. You can also swipe the message: right Safe, up Check First, left Scam.

Every message, link, and phone number in this game is made up. Use 555 numbers and example-style links only as practice.
