Phish or Legit Inbox

Can you spot the traps in a fake inbox before they fool you?

  • 12 practice emails arrive one at a time, and a short timer is running.
  • Tap any suspicious part: a fake sender address, an urgent or threatening tone, an odd link, a request for a password or payment, or a name that doesn’t match the address.
  • Then tap Phish if it is a trick, or Legit if it looks genuine. Real messages have nothing to flag — if nothing looks off, leave it unmarked and choose Legit.
  • You score points for correct clues and the right verdict, plus a speed bonus and a streak bonus. After each email, a tip explains the tells.
  • On a keyboard: L or ← Legit, P or → Phish. Press Enter to start.

Every email, link, and phone number in this game is made up. The companies are fictional, and links use example-style domains only as practice.
